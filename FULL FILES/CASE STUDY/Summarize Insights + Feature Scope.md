#### 💡 **Insights from Research:**

- Existing apps are mostly western-focused or urban-centric.
    
- There’s a huge digital gap in how Indian patients and families access palliative care.
    
- Simpler UI/UX and personalized care tracking is a big need.
    
- Volunteers and family members need to be looped into care activities in real-time.

### 🛠️ **Feature Scope of Your App**

|Module|Features|
|---|---|

|👨‍👩‍👧 **Family Module**|View patient updates, remote alerts, weekly reports|
