Even though existing apps are useful, they have several **shortcomings**, especially for use in Indian rural and semi-urban contexts:

#### 🔴 **Common Pain Points:**

|Pain Point|Description|
|---|---|
|🕒 **Lack of Real-Time Communication**|Most apps don’t support live chat or emergency requests to care volunteers.|
|🌐 **Poor Accessibility in Rural Areas**|Many apps assume stable internet and tech-savvy users.|
|🗂️ **Fragmented Features**|Users must switch between apps for medication, pain tracking, and scheduling help.|
|❌ **No Personalization**|No way to customize care based on disease type or patient condition.|
|🧓 **Not Elder-Friendly**|Complex UI that’s hard for senior citizens to use independently.|
|🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Caregiver Tracking Not Robust**|No proper monitoring of volunteer response time or follow-up.|
